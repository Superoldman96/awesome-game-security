NullCS is a research system that analyzes Counter-Strike 2 demo files and ranks players by how strongly their tick-level behavior warrants human review, with supporting evidence rather than automatic bans. It parses local .dem replays through demoparser2 and awpy into per-engagement windows, then applies a convolutional neural network on short aim, mouse, visibility, and timing sequences before an XGBoost model aggregates roughly 450 per-player features into lobby rankings. The stack is primarily Python for parsing, feature engineering, training, and inference, complemented by a Next.js web UI and FastAPI-style worker for upload and desktop analysis workflows. It targets analysts, tournament admins, and game-security researchers who need transparent, on-machine demo screening and benchmarked review signals, explicitly as a complement to—not a replacement for—production anti-cheat enforcement.
